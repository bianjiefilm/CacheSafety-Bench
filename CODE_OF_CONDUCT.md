# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be useful, rigorous, and welcoming. Contributors are expected to:

- be respectful and specific
- focus on reproducible evidence
- discuss safety tradeoffs in good faith
- avoid personal attacks, harassment, and discriminatory behavior

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, issues, datasets, or pull requests that violate this code of conduct.

Serious or repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent bans from project spaces.
